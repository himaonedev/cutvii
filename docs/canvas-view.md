# Canvas view zoom

Preview has a zoom menu, plus/minus buttons, and Fit. Ctrl/Cmd + mouse wheel or trackpad pinch over the preview zooms around the pointer, from 25% to 400% of the fitted canvas size. The timeline keeps its separate Ctrl/Cmd + wheel shortcut. Middle-mouse drag pans the canvas; Fit returns to 100% and centres it. View settings do not modify clip scale, project resolution, keyframes or exported output.
