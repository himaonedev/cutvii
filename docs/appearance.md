# Appearance

Open **Appearance** in the editor header, next to Export, or in Home Settings. Both menus offer the same 12 one-click dark palettes: Orange, Ocean Blue, Violet, Mint, Rose, Graphite, Gold, Cyan, Emerald, Crimson, Sunset and Coffee.

Buttons, focus rings, sliders, selection outlines and timeline clip colours update immediately. The choice is saved locally and restored before the first editor render. It does not edit projects, media, subtitle colours or exported output.

Dynamic Trio and its custom colour controls have been removed. Previously saved custom choices open with Ocean Blue; existing preset choices are retained.
